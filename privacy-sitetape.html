Rise & Run — Privacy Policy

Rise & Run does not collect any data. The only thing it saves is the last numbers you typed on each drawing (stair rise and run; rafter span, pitch and overhang; roof pitch), so a drawing opens where you left it. Those numbers are saved on your device and are never sent anywhere.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. The unlock is bought through Apple; we never see your payment details.

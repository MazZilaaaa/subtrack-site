SubTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

SubTrack does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code in the app.

  • Your subscription list is stored on your device.
  • If iCloud backup is on, a copy is stored in your own private iCloud (Apple CloudKit). We cannot see it.
  • Reminders are local notifications made on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see payment details.
  • CSV export creates a file only when you tap Export, and you choose where it goes.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to sell, share or lose.

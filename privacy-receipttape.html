ReceiptTape — Privacy Policy

ReceiptTape does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code.

The camera is used only when you tap to snap a receipt. Receipt photos are saved on your device. Text is read from them on your device by Apple's built-in Vision framework. No photo, no text and no total ever leaves your iPhone. The app makes no network requests.

Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

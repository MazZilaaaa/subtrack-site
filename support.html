SubTrack — Support

My reminders do not arrive

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → SubTrack and allow notifications. SubTrack also shows an orange banner in the app when reminders are off.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the paywall and tap “Restore purchases”. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID.

How do I get my data out?

Tap the share icon → Export CSV. Everything, including archived items, is in the file.
